About us

Three people who automate and build digital presence.

Motex was born from a simple belief: local shops and small businesses deserve the same tools as larger companies. Smart flows that save time and websites that open doors. No intermediaries, no inflated quotes, with the founders on the phone.

Who we are

A Spanish digital startup for companies and shops that want to grow.

Motex is a young project with a clear proposal: bring AI automation and professional web design to those who need it most: freelancers, shops and SMEs that do not have the time or budget to hire a large consultancy.

We work fully remotely from Spain. We do not have an office because we do not need one: we use that efficiency to keep prices honest and spend more time with each client.

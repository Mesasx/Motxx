Context

A technical engineering company in Madrid.

Industrial technical engineering company in Madrid, approximately 8 people. The objective was clear from the start: before recommending any tool or proposing any change, we had to understand the company from the inside: its structure, technology environment and the real experience of the people who work there.

The result was a complete diagnosis audit: a study based on team feedback, operational environment analysis and identification of the elements, digital or not, where a change would have the greatest impact. All of it was captured in a main automation plan and a documented roadmap.

Methodology

How the study was built.

The starting point was not technology, it was the company. Before proposing any change, we gathered the real perspective of the people working inside and built a structured analysis in four steps.

Step 1

Understand the environment

Analysis of the existing technology and operational environment: tools in use, documented processes, real workflows and team structure. The basis for understanding what works and where friction appears.

Step 2

Collect internal feedback

Survey distributed to the whole team to understand their view on general satisfaction, working conditions, interdepartmental communication and perception of the technology used day to day. Eight responses collected and analyzed.

Step 3

AI-assisted analysis

Processing responses with AI: eNPS calculation, assessment by critical areas, identification of friction patterns and detection of improvement opportunities not obvious at first glance.

Step 4

Prepare the main plan

Using the study data, we prepared an automation plan and structured guide: priority areas, recommended changes, technological or not, and a phased roadmap for progressive implementation.

Who we are

A family restaurant for eating the old-fashioned way.

La Encina keeps a recognizable menu for locals, families and visitors who want clear dishes and good service. The website focuses on quick questions: what to eat, how much it costs, which allergens each dish contains and how to book.

The tone is friendly and classic, with large buttons and visible details so any audience can call, check the menu or find opening hours easily.

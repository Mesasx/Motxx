Motex Campus · in development

Our own space to learn AI with structure, practice and follow-up.

We are developing Motex Campus: a private platform where every user who buys a course will have their own learning space, with videos, classes, tests, support materials and progress tracking.

Alongside recorded content and activities, each student will have a weekly mentoring session to resolve questions, review progress and learn how to apply AI to real work: prompts, automations, productivity, documentation and safe usage criteria.

The campus will be enabled from July 2026. Enrollment is already open for users who want to reserve a place before launch.

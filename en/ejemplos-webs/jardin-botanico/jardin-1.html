About the garden

Science, beauty and life on every path.

The Model Botanical Garden was founded in 1892 to study, conserve and share the planet’s flora. Today it hosts more than 3,000 species organized into themed collections that reflect the diversity of the plant kingdom.

Our gardens are a living laboratory, a peaceful space and an educational resource for schools, universities and plant lovers.

Our collections

Twelve plant worlds, one garden.

Organized by geographic origin, botanical family and ecosystem, our collections offer a journey through the planet’s diversity.

🌵

Xerophytic plants

Cacti and succulents from arid environments around the world. More than 400 species.

🌿

Mediterranean flora

Vegetation typical of our climate: aromatic plants, scrubland and holm oak woods.

🌺

Tropical plants

The greenhouse houses 600 species from tropical and subtropical zones.

🍂

Historic trees

Centuries-old trees, some over 300 years old. A living heritage.

🌾

Ethnobotanical garden

Plants used for medicine, food and industry throughout history.

🦋

Pollinator garden

Designed to attract bees, butterflies and other beneficial insects.

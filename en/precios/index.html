FAQ

Why so affordable?

Because we have just started and need case studies. We will reduce launch perks when we have a portfolio, not technical quality.

Do you have real cases?

Yes. We completed a diagnosis audit for a technical engineering company in Madrid: environment analysis, team surveys, AI-assisted study and delivered automation plan. You can see it in Cases.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, from Spain. We do not have a physical office: we invest that cost in training, tools and pricing.

What happens to my data?

We host flows on European VPS (Hetzner, Germany). GDPR. Data processing agreement signed with each client.

What if I change CRM or email?

We adapt the flows. Well-built automations are agnostic to the specific tool.

Can I modify the flows myself afterwards?

Yes, we leave documentation and training. If you want full autonomy, the Growth package covers it.

How long does a project take?

1-2 weeks for a simple flow; 3-6 weeks for a package; 6-12 weeks for a custom industrial project.

What happens if something fails?

First month of maintenance included. Guarantee: if it does not work as agreed within 30 days, we fix it for free or do not charge that part.

Do you charge hourly or by project?

By fixed project. Extra hours only in advanced maintenance.

What do I need to start?

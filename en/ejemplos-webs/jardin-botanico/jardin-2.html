Our story

A century and a quarter caring for life.

The Model Botanical Garden was founded in 1892 as part of an ambitious scientific research project. Over the years it has become a reference space for plant conservation, scientific outreach and public enjoyment.

1892
Foundation of the garden with the first Iberian flora collections.
1934
Opening of the tropical greenhouse, the largest in the region.
1978
Opening to the public and creation of the school education program.
2010
Renovation of the ethnobotanical garden and pollinator garden.
Collections

The diversity of the plant kingdom.

Every corner of the garden is a different ecosystem. Discover them at your own pace or with one of our guides.

🌵

Xerophytes and cacti

More than 400 species adapted to aridity across different continents.

🌴

Tropical greenhouse

600 tropical-zone species in a controlled microclimate of 26 C and 80% humidity.

🌸

Mediterranean flora

Aromatic plants, scrubland, holm oak woodland and native Mediterranean-basin flora.

🌿

Ethnobotanical garden

Plants used for medicine, cooking and industry throughout human history.

🦋

Pollinator garden

A garden designed to attract bees, butterflies and other beneficial insects.

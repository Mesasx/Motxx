About us

Who leads this project?

Motex is led by Pedro, with a Computer Engineering background and practical specialization in applied artificial intelligence. The project exists to bring practical AI to small businesses, freelancers and local companies that want to save time, reduce repetitive work and operate with more efficient systems without depending on a large consultancy.

Who I am

I am Pedro, with a Computer Engineering background and specialized in AI since 2023.

My background starts with my university studies in Computer Engineering and a practical specialization in artificial intelligence that began in 2023. Since then, I have dedicated hundreds of hours to testing models, tools, APIs, agents, automations and courses focused on AI applied to real work, supported especially by training, documentation and technical resources from Anthropic, Google Gemini and OpenAI.

At the beginning of 2026 I turned that experience into Motex: a professional project created to help small businesses understand what AI can do for them and, above all, bring it into concrete processes that save time, money and operational effort.

Why Motex exists

AI is moving very fast, but many companies are barely using a small part of its potential.

In a society where everything changes quickly, many AI tools have entered daily work only superficially: they are used to write a text, summarize something specific or answer a question, but they are not truly integrated into work processes.

Motex was born from that observation: most businesses are not using anything close to what AI can offer. In practice, many companies use only a small part of its potential, often no more than 5%, while manual tasks, repeated emails, duplicated documents, slow quotes, hand-made reports and person-dependent processes remain in place.

Current stage

Tools, automation and duplication

High priority
High priority

Unify the document management system and eliminate duplication

Diagnosis: Several employees fill in the same information in different documents. An internal management tool is mentioned, as well as the need for everyone to use the same templates. Duplication is the main operational pain point.
  • 1
    Audit current forms and templates: detect what information is duplicated and between which departments.
  • 2
    Select or adapt a single management system that consolidates quality, environment and operations.
  • 3
    Create standard mandatory corporate templates for the whole company, integrated into the same platform.
  • 4
    Train all teams before the official launch of the new system.
Recommended tools
SharePoint + Power Automate
Document management + automatic flows · Microsoft 365
Notion
Wiki + templates + centralized knowledge base
Zapier / Make
Automate flows between systems without coding
High priority

Automate time tracking, work reports and repetitive administrative tasks

Diagnosis: Time tracking and reports appear in several responses as tasks that consume unnecessary time. Invoicing and work reports are also mentioned.
  • 1
    Implement a digital clock-in app with geolocation or NFC that automatically sends data to the payroll system.
  • 2
    Automate work report generation with data already present in the project system.
  • 3
    Integrate invoicing with the ERP so project data flows automatically into invoices.
Recommended tools
Factorial HR
Time tracking, absences, reports · Spanish solution
Sesame HR
Mobile app and automatic reporting · Spanish
Holded
ERP + invoicing + integrated projects · Spanish
Medium priority

Provide a second screen for technical roles

Diagnosis: One employee expressly points to the lack of a large second screen as an efficiency limitation when working with several documents simultaneously.
  • 1
    Identify which roles (technical, projects, drafting) require dual screens and quantify the cost.
  • 2
    Purchase additional monitors of at least 24" for those roles in the next 3 months.
  • 3
    Include dual screens as standard equipment for new technical roles.
04

Artificial intelligence and digital training

AI · Strategy
High priority

Launch a training program in business-applied AI

Diagnosis: 87% already use AI tools but 100% need training. Main uses: report writing, regulation search, summaries and data analysis. High motivation: several answer "yes, a lot" when asked about interest in training.
  • 1
    Create an "AI pioneers" group with advanced users to identify the 5 highest-impact use cases in the company.
  • 2
    Organize a 4-hour practical workshop: technical report writing, regulation search and AI-assisted data analysis.
  • 3
    Purchase corporate licenses to avoid free accounts with limitations and no data privacy.
  • 4
    Create an internal library of useful prompts for the company’s most common tasks.
Recommended tools
Microsoft Copilot 365
AI integrated into Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams
Claude for Work
Ideal for technical reports and regulation analysis
Perplexity for Teams
Smart regulation search with cited sources
Autodesk AI
Automation of repetitive tasks in CAD

Internal communication and coordination

Communication
High priority

Establish a structured communication channel between departments

Diagnosis: Interdepartmental communication is the second lowest-rated area. Lack of coordination in quality and environment, chaotic reception of client information and lack of process alignment are mentioned.
Communications related to the quality management system and the environmental management system.
That all departments use the same templates and the same management system.

Medium priority

Improve work recognition and communication with managers

Diagnosis: Work recognition is the lowest-rated day-to-day aspect (3.0/5). Communication with the direct manager also scores low (3.3/5). Direct impact on satisfaction and retention.
  • 1

  • 2
    Create an internal recognition system: mention in team meeting, public message in Teams, etc.
  • 3
    Ultra-short quarterly climate survey (3 questions) to monitor progress.

Immediate improvement of restrooms and air conditioning

Diagnosis: Restrooms are the lowest-rated item in the entire survey (1.2/5). Air conditioning also scores very low (3.0/5). Several employees mention it emphatically in open responses.
Restrooms IN PROPER CONDITION.
Better air conditioning.
  • 1
    Restrooms: Immediate review of condition and maintenance. Increase cleaning frequency and permanently provide basic supplies.
  • 2
    Air conditioning: Review the HVAC system. If it is insufficient, prioritize repair before summer.
  • 3
    Establish a maintenance issue reporting protocol so any employee can report problems.
Medium priority

Create a meeting / video conference room and improve acoustics

Diagnosis: The video conference room is the most requested resource (6 out of 8 people). The noise level (2.6/5) is low because external calls interfere with colleagues.
  • 1
    Enable a dedicated video call space, even if by adapting an existing area with acoustic panels.
  • 2
    Provide a quality screen, camera and microphone for client presentations.
  • 3
    Install acoustic dividers in the open work area to reduce interference.
  • 4
    Add lockers or personal storage for each employee (requested by 3 people).

Professional development and career

Talent
Medium priority

Define a career plan and clear growth prospects

Diagnosis: Professional development prospects score 3.6/5, with one employee rating them a 2. There are explicit mentions of wanting more growth opportunities.
  • 1
    Conduct individual development interviews to understand aspirations and draw up a 1-2 year plan.
  • 2
    Set clear, measurable annual goals linked to compensation reviews.
  • 3
    Create an annual external training budget per person (sector courses, certifications).
  • 4
    Actively communicate strategic projects so employees see the future and their role in it.
